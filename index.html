ABOUT

Hi there! I'm currently a fourth-year Computer & Information Science PhD student at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Lyle Ungar and Sharath Chandra Guntuku. I also consult for the World Bank's Development Impact Edutainment Program.

My research spans computational social science and public health. Lately, I've been interested in human–AI interaction, with a focus on designing systems that support health behavior change.

More than anything, I'm excited by research that can help inform public policy. My work has appeared in the New York Times, CNN, and the Washington Post, and has been cited by the Ohio governor, Kamala Harris, and Elizabeth Warren.

PREVIOUSLY

Before beginning my PhD at Penn, I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Statistics. I spent time as a visiting researcher at various institutions and worked with computational epidemiology teams analyzing public messaging dynamics.
